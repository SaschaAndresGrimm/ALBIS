**Everything in Its Right Place:** the mask controls move to where they act, the mask colours become one simple row, and Mask saturated works on TIFF and CBF images.

### Fixed

- **Mask saturated can be switched on TIFF and CBF images.** It stayed greyed
  out and unticked even though saturated pixels were already being masked.

### Changed

- **Mask colours are one row of chips under the colour bar.** Gap, Defective
  and Saturated, each showing its colour and dimmed when the image has no such
  pixel. Click one to choose its colour from a small set:
  - colours that stand out on the current colour map;
  - the colour map's own low end, middle and high end, to make a class blend
    in, for example gaps into the background;
  - **Auto**, which goes back to the colour chosen for the map.
- **Apply mask and Mask saturated are in the Histogram section**, next to the
  colour map, instead of under View → Advanced view controls.
- **Pixel values is a toolbar button**: **123**, next to the magnifier.
- **ALBULA HDR is the first colour map in the list.**

### Removed

- **Make one from frames…** in the Flatfield section. Combine Frames, in the
  same tab, does the same, and its **Use as flat** still makes the result the
  flat in one click.
