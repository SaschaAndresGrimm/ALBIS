Two more ways to fill detector gaps, noise that makes filled pixels look measured, and two fixes for typing levels and opening files on a Mac.

### Added

- **Two more inpainting methods.** **Interpolate along edges** carries an
  edge crossing a module gap straight through it, instead of turning it into
  a blurred step or filling a wide gap as stripes. **Nearest value** gives
  each hole the value of the nearer side of its gap: sharp and never blurred.
  Both are in the Inpainting method menu and in exports, and the viewer and
  exports fill identically.
- **Poisson noise for inpainted pixels.** **Add Poisson noise** makes the
  filled pixels as noisy as the measured ones, so a filled gap no longer
  stands out as smooth. The same frame always gets the same noise, in the
  image and in exports. Off by default; exported files say the noise was
  added.

### Fixed

- **ALBIS is offered for your files on macOS again.** Another viewer, ALBULA
  among them, can claim `.h5`, `.cbf`, `.tif` or `.edf` files with a type of
  its own, and ALBIS would then not be offered for them. ALBIS now also
  claims `.h5`, `.hdf5`, `.cbf` and `.edf` by extension, still as an
  alternative that never takes the default.
- **Background and foreground take the value you type.** A value was clamped
  to the current frame's range, so a fixed range across a series could not be
  set. Only the order is kept now, background below foreground.

---

Update if you fill gaps and want the new methods or noise, if you type
contrast levels, or if you use ALBIS on a Mac alongside other viewers.
Nothing has moved; the new options sit with the existing inpainting settings.
