# ALBIS 0.10.2: Mind The Gap (Spacing)

0.10.1 taught the app some manners. 0.10.2 teaches the ROI profiles some physics: the line and radial profiles now speak resolution. Draw a profile across your diffraction and read d-spacing straight off the plot — peaks labeled, axis included, units your choice. A small patch with a satisfyingly direct payoff.

## Highlights

- **A resolution axis on your profiles.** ROI line and radial (circle/annulus) profiles gained a second x-axis up top that maps pixels/radius to d-spacing — styled to match the existing axes, not bolted on. It shows up only when the geometry is calibrated (distance, pixel size, energy), so it never lies about numbers it can't compute.
- **Peaks that label themselves.** A lightweight 1D peak finder walks the profile, marks the prominent features, and tags each with its resolution. The diffraction rings under your line/annulus now come with their d-spacing written on.
- **d (Å) or Q (1/nm) — your call.** A unit toggle lives in the profile's ⚙ menu (default d, with Q = 2π/d). Flip it and both the axis ticks and the peak labels follow. Bonus: Q is linear in reciprocal space, so its ticks come out evenly spaced instead of bunching up at high resolution.
- **One-click beam centering.** Radial d-spacing is only exact when the ring is centered on the beam. The new **Center on beam** button for circle/annulus ROIs snaps the center onto the beam center, so the resolution axis is correct without pixel-hunting by hand.

## Under The Hood

- The d-spacing math reuses the existing resolution-ring geometry (planar Bragg and full panel-geometry modes), so the profile axis agrees with the overlay rings.
- Conversions are display-only and the peak/axis work is rAF-throttled — toggling units is a redraw, not a recompute, and the new code adds no measurable cost to the profile pipeline.
- All four new strings are localized across the full set of 13 locales.
