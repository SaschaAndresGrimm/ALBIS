A macOS fix, and the reason to take it: **if you installed 0.20.0 from the
`.dmg`, please reinstall from this one.**

### Fixed

- **The app inside the macOS DMG had no notarization ticket stapled to it.**
  0.20.0 signed the app, built the DMG, notarized the DMG, and *then* stapled
  the app — by which point the DMG had already sealed an unstapled copy. macOS
  then had to ask Apple for a ticket on every launch instead of checking
  offline. On a fast connection you would never notice; on a slow or filtered
  one ALBIS simply never opens and Force Quit is the only way out.

  The app is now notarized and stapled *before* the DMG is built, and the
  release check mounts the DMG and verifies the app it actually ships — which
  is the check that was missing, and why 0.20.0 passed every gate.

  The `.zip` builds were never affected. Neither were Linux, Windows or Docker.

### Added

- The two update settings from 0.20.0, `allow_update_download` and
  `allow_update_apply`, now have checkboxes in **Settings → Connection**
  alongside "Check for updates on startup", instead of being config-file-only.
  A managed deployment can still pin them from the environment, and the fields
  show as locked when it does.

---

Grabbing the right file matters here: the release page lists an Intel and an
Apple Silicon `.dmg`, four characters apart. **Help → Check for Updates** picks
it for you.
