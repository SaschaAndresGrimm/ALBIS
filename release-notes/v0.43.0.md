**It's Dangerous to Go Alone! Take This.**: the old man's gift in The Legend of Zelda, handed over before the journey starts. 0.43.0 is the first ALBIS release with an outside contributor: Lucas Wagner wrote the Min/Max Finder cutoffs and the clickable **Manual axes** tag. He worked with an AI coding agent that followed ALBIS's contributor guides, checks and review steps. Both pull requests arrived complete, with tests, translations and documentation, and needed only small fixes before merging.

### Added

- **The Min/Max Finder can ignore pixels below or above a value.** Two
  optional boxes, **Ignore below** and **Ignore above**, leave out pixels
  outside the range you set. On a frame full of zeros, enter 1 under
  **Ignore below** and **Min** lists the lowest pixels that read anything,
  instead of the first of the zeros. Left empty, nothing changes; the CSV
  export records the cutoffs it used.

### Changed

- **One click on an ROI plot's Manual axes tag returns it to automatic
  axes.** The tag then reads **Auto axes**, and a second click brings back
  the axes you had. Until now the only way back was the plot's settings.
  Hover over the tag to see what a click does.

### Fixed

- **Numbers in messages are grouped the way the interface language writes
  them:** 5.000.000 in German, 5,000,000 in English, whatever the computer's
  region is set to.
- **The translations use one word for each technical term.** A detector
  frame was a "Frame" in one German message and a "Bild" in the next, and
  the same in several other languages. About 250 strings in twelve languages
  now follow the translation glossary.
- **A sum of data from a detector on a 2θ arm, such as I19-2's, opens with
  its beam centre and rings in the right place.** Sums made with earlier
  versions still open off-centre; make them again.
- **Fit to Window keeps the image clear of the menu bar, the toolbar, the
  footer and the side panel,** and an opened file starts out fitted the
  same way.

---

Everyone can update; files and settings are unchanged. Anyone who works in
a language other than English will see clearer wording and number grouping.
Anyone who sums 2θ-arm data should remake those sums. The Min/Max Finder's
new boxes are under **Pixels to find**.
