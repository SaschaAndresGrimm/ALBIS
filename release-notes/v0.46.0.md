**Enhance 224 to 176**: the ROI plots learn to zoom, pan and go back the way you would expect, down to single pixels, and linked windows can now step and play through frames together.

### Added

- **Move around an ROI plot more easily.**
  - Drag on an axis to pan along that axis only.
  - **Shift-drag** a box to zoom into it.
  - On a trackpad, pinch to zoom and swipe sideways to pan along x.
  - **↶ Previous view** in the plot's header goes back after a zoom, pan
    or reset.
  - Double-clicking an axis resets just that axis.
- **See which point you are reading.** Hovering marks the sample under the
  tooltip, and a profile zoomed in far enough shows a dot on every pixel.
- **Linked windows step and play together.** The link menu has a new
  **Frame** option: stepping or **Play** in one window moves the others by the
  same number of frames, keeping the gap between them.

### Changed

- **Round numbers on the ROI plot axes**: 0, 0.02, 0.04 instead of 0, 0.043,
  0.087. The histogram's bars line up with their bin edges and follow the
  light and dark theme.
- **The macOS disk image shows the ALBIS icon** when mounted.

### Fixed

- **Wheel zoom on an ROI plot stays smooth on a short range.** On a few
  pixels it used to zoom in jumps, could get stuck zooming out, and slid
  sideways; the point under the pointer now stays put.
- **The x axis titles of the ROI plots** are no longer cut off at the bottom.
- **Updating a remote ALBIS** downloads the installer to your own computer,
  and no longer offers a **Show in Folder** button that opened on the
  server's screen.

---

Everyone can update; nothing changes in how files are read or exported. If you
work with ROI profiles, try Shift-drag and **↶ Previous view** first. The full
list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/SaschaAndresGrimm/ALBIS/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
