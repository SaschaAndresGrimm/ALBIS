# ALBIS 0.10.4: Spot On

The peak finder went back to crystallography school. It still runs the same single fast pass over the full-resolution frame — no GPU, no downsampling, no waiting — but the spots it hands you are cleaner, better placed, and finally come with the numbers you actually care about.

## Highlights

- **No more chasing zingers.** A footprint check throws out lone hot pixels and cosmic-ray spikes before they ever reach the list — a real Bragg spot has shoulders, a zinger doesn't.
- **Sub-pixel centroids.** Peaks now land on their intensity-weighted centre of mass instead of just the brightest pixel — the sub-pixel positions indexing wants, essentially for free.
- **Sees spots on the diagonal.** Local-maxima detection went from 4- to 8-connected, so spots hiding behind a diagonal neighbour stop slipping through, and flat plateaus stop getting counted twice.
- **SNR, right in the table.** Every peak now shows its local signal-to-noise — the one number that answers "is this actually real?" It was already being computed; we just stopped throwing it away.
- **Resolution per spot.** With detector geometry set, the list gains a **d (Å)** column so you can read each reflection's resolution shell at a glance. Nudge the distance or beam center and it updates instantly — no re-scan.

## Under The Hood

- SNR and d-spacing are basically free: SNR is the score the finder already ranks by, and resolution is computed only for the handful of peaks that make the final cut.
- The **d (Å)** column hides itself when there's no geometry, so it never clutters the view.
- Still one linear pass, start to finish — same speed you're used to, just smarter about what it keeps.
