**Two-Dimensional Thinking**: a release that adds a dimension. A box ROI can now be shown in 3D, and on a Mac an update finishes in one click.

### Added

- **3D View of a box ROI.** Tick **3D View** under the box's profiles in
  **Statistics and ROI**, and the box becomes a surface whose height is the
  pixel value, in the image's colour map, contrast and mask colours, like
  adxv's 3-D magnify window. It shows a reflection's whole shape at once: one
  peak or a split one, a saturated flat top, a lone hot pixel. Drag to turn
  it, Shift-drag to move it, scroll to zoom and Shift+scroll to stretch the
  heights. **Wire mesh** and **Log height** are one tick away, hovering gives
  the pixel and its value, and the view saves as a PNG like the other plots.
  With **Whole frame** it shows the whole detector in blocks, each as high as
  its highest pixel, so a single spot still stands out.
- **Quit and Open Disk Image.** On a Mac, once **Download Update** has
  fetched the new version, one button opens the disk image and quits ALBIS;
  drag ALBIS to Applications and start it again. Before, Finder refused to
  replace an app that was still running, and nothing said why. The Windows
  portable archive gets **Quit and Show in Explorer** for the same reason.

### Documentation

- The user guide's pictures are made again for the current interface, with
  a new one of the 3D view.

Anyone who looks at spots should update: the 3D view needs no setup beyond a
box ROI and a browser with WebGL2, which current browsers have. The full list
