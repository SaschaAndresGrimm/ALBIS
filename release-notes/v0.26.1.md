**Glow Up:** the help page and About take on the start screen's look.

### Changed

- **The help page (F1) is easier to read and find your way in.** The wave
  picture runs across the top as a header, with the ALBIS wordmark, the version
  and the DECTRIS wordmark. A contents column stays beside the text and marks the
  section you are reading. Text sits at a comfortable width in the app's own
  font, keys look like keys, and each section links to more in the User Guide. A
  short section introduces the beta Detector tab.
- **About is lighter.** A banner of the wave picture with the ALBIS wordmark
  replaces the large icon, and the DECTRIS logo is white on the dark card, as on
  the start screen.

---

Update for the new look of the help page and About. Nothing changes in how
ALBIS opens or shows your data, and the help page still loads nothing from the
internet.
