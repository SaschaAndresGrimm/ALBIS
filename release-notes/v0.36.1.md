**Fast Forward:** the same ALBIS, quicker. Stepping through frames, live view, playback and exports all take less time, and every image and file comes out exactly as before.

### Changed

- **Stepping through frames and live view are quicker.** A new frame's
  statistics, histogram and auto contrast take 5 ms instead of 22 ms for a
  1 Mpx frame, and 30 ms instead of 100 ms for a 4M one.
- **Flatfield and inpainting in the viewer take about 40% less time** per
  frame: 18 instead of 29 ms for a 1 Mpx frame with both on, 85 instead of
  147 ms for a 4M one.
- **Playback starts at 5 fps and goes up to 30 fps**, instead of 1 to 10.
  If the machine cannot keep up with the speed chosen, playback simply runs
  as fast as it can.
- **Exports with inpainting are faster** on machines that convert one to
  four frames at a time, by 15–30%.
- **Exports on Apple silicon use only the performance cores**: just as
  fast, with more room left for the viewer and live data while an export
  runs.

Update if you step through, play back or watch large frames live, especially
