**Name Dropper:** ALBIS remembers the detectors you connect to, by name, so you no longer have to recall which IP belongs to which detector.

### Added

- **Known detectors under the address field.** Every detector that answers,
  whether you connect in the beta Detector tab or test or start the SIMPLON
  monitor in the Data tab, is remembered with its model and serial number.
  There is no save step. Click into the address field, or press ▾ or ↓, and
  pick a detector to connect to it; typing narrows the list by name, address,
  model or serial. Both address fields offer the same list.
- **Keep, name and remove detectors.** ☆ keeps a detector at the top of the
  list; the ☆ beside a connected detector's name does the same. ✎ gives it a
  name of your own, such as "Lab 2 – P4". × removes it. The others are the last
  eight used.
- **A detector keeps its entry when its address changes.** It is recognised by
  its serial number, so a detector at a new address keeps its name and star
  instead of appearing twice.

---

Update if you connect to the same few detectors again and again. The addresses
earlier versions suggested are carried over into the new list. The list stays
in your browser with the other ALBIS settings.
