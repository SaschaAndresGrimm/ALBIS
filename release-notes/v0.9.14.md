# ALBIS 0.9.14: Happy Birthday, Tilman

This is the linked-window release.

Tilman has been asking for synchronized viewer windows for quite a while. It is now here, it is live, and it has arrived just in time to make side-by-side image comparison feel much less like synchronized swimming performed by hand.

## Highlights

- Viewer windows can now be linked across ALBIS browser windows.
- Zoom and image-space position stay synchronized, including while panning and zooming live.
- Zoomed-out views now keep the same image-space center instead of drifting apart when the image is smaller than the window.
- Sync options are selectable from the link-button menu.
- Position, Contrast, and ROI sync are all enabled by default.
- Contrast sync includes levels, auto-scale state, colormap, and invert mode.
- ROI sync shares the selected region geometry for line, box, circle, and annulus selections.

## Why This Matters

Comparing two detector images should be about the images, not about nudging two windows into reluctant agreement.

Open two viewers, link them, and let ALBIS keep the view state together while you inspect changes, compare frames, or check whether the interesting bit really moved.
