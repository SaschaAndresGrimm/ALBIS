**Ain't No Mountain High Enough**: ALBIS finds a frame's highest and lowest pixels, counts what lies below, between and above the contrast levels, and lets the update notice switch itself off. Thank you for the feature requests, Lucas!

### Added

- **Min/Max Finder.** A new Analysis tool lists the highest pixels, the
  lowest, or both, with position, value and mask value, marks them on the
  image, and exports them as CSV. When many pixels share the extreme value,
  such as 0 on a quiet frame, it says how many. It sits in a new **General**
  group in **Settings → Tools**.
- **Pixels below, between and above the levels.** **Statistics and ROI**
  counts the pixels **Below BG**, from **BG to FG** and **Above FG**, with
  their share, for the ROI or the whole frame. The labels name the levels,
  the counts follow BG and FG as you drag them, and the ROI's CSV export
  includes them.
- **The update notice can switch itself off.** It has a **Don't check for
  updates at startup** box, the same switch as in **Settings → Connection**.

### Changed

- **A new session starts with a Box ROI** instead of a Line ROI.
- **The Getting started example data is one zip file.**

### Fixed

- **A box ROI's profile no longer goes blank after the box is moved** while
  its axes are pinned.
