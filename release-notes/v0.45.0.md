**Bring Balance to the Force**: ALBIS now has a light side as well as a dark one, and the whole window has been reworked: the image gets the room, the side panel sits beside it, and the controls are quieter.

### Added

- **A light theme.** **Settings → Viewer → Appearance**: Dark (the default),
  Light, or System. Once an image is open, the area around it stays dark grey
  in both, so faint signal keeps its contrast.

### Changed

- **A new layout.** One header row, the side panel docked on the right, and a
  full-width timeline under the image for scrubbing long series. The status
  line shows the pixel under the mouse.
- **A quieter interface.** Flat surfaces, filled fields with the unit inside,
  and amber for anything that needs a look. The start screen lists your recent
  files.
- **Command palette and Settings.** Recent commands come first, and Settings
  is only as tall as it needs to be.

### Fixed

- **Auto contrast gives a readable first view.** Sparse frames no longer open
  solid white, and masked pixels no longer set the levels.
- **No jump at startup.** The image no longer resizes as the window opens.

---

Everyone can update; nothing changes in how files are read or exported. **Open
menu** is now the panel button at the right end of the header, the frame
controls are in the timeline under the image, and **Copy build info** is in
**Help → About**. The full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/SaschaAndresGrimm/ALBIS/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
