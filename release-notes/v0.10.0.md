# ALBIS 0.10.0: Grab It And Drag It

The last few releases taught ALBIS to talk back and sync its windows. This one hands you the steering wheel. Resolution rings are now things you grab and drag, the peak finder learned to tell signal from a hot module sulking on a high background, and your image series can finally leave the building as an animated GIF. Minor version bump, major amount of pointing-and-clicking.

## Highlights

- **Drag the rings. Drag the beam.** Resolution rings are no longer read-only decorations. Grab the beam-center marker to reposition it, or pull a ring in or out to change its d-spacing — right on the canvas, with a proper grab cursor and handle highlight. The geometry input fields stay the single source of truth, so everything validates and redraws the way it always did.
- **Geometry that stays put on live sources.** Manually corrected the distance, center, or energy while a SIMPLON / remote / JUNGFRAUJOCH stream is running? It used to get steamrolled by the very next frame. Now editing any geometry field engages a lock scoped to that source, with a Live/Locked pill to show you what's going on and a "Reset to live" button to snap back. The geometry inputs also moved out of their collapsible hideout to the top level.
- **A peak finder that isn't fooled by bright noise.** The finder now ranks spots by *local* signal-to-noise instead of raw brightness, estimating background from an annulus around each candidate. Beam stops and hot modules stop hogging the list; faint-but-real reflections get a seat. New **Min. SNR** control (default 5; set it to 0 for the old intensity ranking).
- **Export Animation → GIF.** Turn an image series or multi-frame dataset into an animated GIF that matches exactly what's on screen — colormap, contrast, invert, mask, saturation and all. Frame range with an every-Nth step, full-image or visible-area region, output scale, fps, and a loop toggle, plus a live size estimate. Find it under File → Export Animation…, the Playback popover's Export GIF…, or ⌘/Ctrl+G.

## Also Fixed

- **No more nuclear-white images.** Summed gap/dead-pixel sentinels (think 65535 × frames) used to form a spike that dragged the auto-contrast percentile into orbit and blew the whole image out to white. Auto contrast now rejects detached extreme-pixel clusters — while carefully leaving your genuine bright Bragg peaks alone.
- **Update checks work in packaged builds again.** Packaged builds now verify TLS against the bundled certifi CA bundle, so the update check stops quietly failing.
- **Tidier ROI plots.** The Line/Annulus/Circle projection plot now respects the same default height as the box and histogram plots instead of stretching down the side panel.

## Under The Hood

- A native-level pass over all 12 locale translations: filled English gaps, fixed mistranslations (the network "Port" was being rendered as a *harbor* in several languages), and cleaned up Swiss German dialect.
- Documentation restructured ahead of 1.0.0: a beginner Getting Started section in the README, and the Developer Guide split into four focused, cross-linked docs (DEVELOPER_GUIDE, ARCHITECTURE, CODE_MAP, API_CONTRACTS).
