**Stayin' Alive:** ALBIS keeps running while a tab is open in the background, and on macOS a stopped ALBIS no longer pretends otherwise.

### Fixed

- **ALBIS no longer stops itself while a tab is open.** The automatic stop
  added in 0.32.0 ends ALBIS 15 minutes after its last tab closed. It could
  also fire with tabs still open. On macOS, a browser window left behind other
  apps can go quiet for that long (App Nap), and ALBIS then stopped under it.
  Now every open tab keeps a connection to ALBIS that stays open however quiet
  the tab is, and the countdown only starts once no tab is connected.
- **On macOS, a stopped ALBIS leaves the Dock and the menu bar.** When ALBIS
  stopped, the app stayed in the Dock and the menu bar as if it were running,
  and the browser could not connect until it was quit and opened again. It now
  quits by itself, and clicking ALBIS starts it afresh.

---

Update if you use ALBIS on a Mac, or keep its tab open in the background on
any system. Nothing else changes.
