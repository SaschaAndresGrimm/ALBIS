**Trigger Happy:** the beta Detector tab is complete. Every SIMPLON setting and command now has its place, a **?** that explains it, and a button that shows what it is doing.

### Changed

- **Commands in one place.** Troubleshooting is now **Commands**: Arm, Trigger,
  Disarm and Cancel series, Re-read settings and Re-initialize, each with a
  **?** saying what it does and its SIMPLON command.
- **Buttons that answer.** Every command button shows a spinner while it runs,
  then "✓ Done" or why it failed, right beside it, with no question popping up
  at the top of the tab. Only **Delete all files**, which cannot be undone,
  takes a second click on the same button.
- **A tidier Data output.** File writer, stream and monitor each show their
  switch, main settings and state. The files on the detector and each output's
  other settings and commands (compression, Initialize file writer, Reset
  stream, Clear buffer, Initialize monitor) are in closed sections. The free
  storage is in the section header, the file writer's data page in its header
  line.
- **Advanced fits on one screen.** Its groups are closed sections with their
  number of settings, the filter opens the ones that match, every setting has
  a **?**, and the goniometer's start and increment share one row per axis.
  Units a detector does not report come from the SIMPLON reference: "150 µm",
  not 0.00015.
- **The sections run** Acquisition, Data output, Commands, Advanced, with
  Activity at the bottom.

### Fixed

- **Disarm and Cancel series no longer hang** while a series runs; a series
  ended this way says "stopped after …".
- **A switch just flipped is no longer reported as "changed by another
  program"** and shown in its old state.
- **A field in Data output no longer loses what you are typing** when the
  status updates.
- **Long durations read as hours:** "10 h", not "600:00 min".

---

Update if you use the beta Detector tab. The recovery steps moved: Reset stream
and Delete all files are in Data output with their outputs, Re-initialize and
the manual series commands under Commands. The tab stays hidden until you switch
it on under Settings → Viewer.
