The beta Detector tab goes from a test series to its data: open the result in ALBIS, take one image or watch continuously, and see before a series whether it will fit.

### Added

- **Open in ALBIS.** After a series, a line sums it up ("Series 15: 1000
  images · 10 s · 2 files, 48 MB"). **Open in ALBIS** copies the series from
  the detector into ALBIS's data folder and opens it: the full data, with every
  tool, not just the live preview.
- **Snap and Continuous.** Next to Acquire: **Snap** takes one image now (1 s),
  whatever the series is set to; **Continuous** shows images live at 10 Hz
  until Stop, for aligning, and saves nothing. Both put the series settings back
  afterwards. Their exposure, rate and longest run are set under Advanced →
  Quick actions.
- **A check before every series.** "✓ Ready · about 33.5 MB with bslz4,
  estimated, 155.2 GB free", or what would make the series fail: not enough
  storage, a name pattern that overwrites the last series, high voltage not
  ready, a threshold above the photon energy, or nothing being saved.
- **Timing you can read:** "100 Hz · 0.1 µs between images".

### Changed

- **The enable trigger modes work as the detector means them.** "Internal,
  enable" and "External, enable" set images per trigger to 1 for you. In
  internal enable, you choose each image's exposure next to Trigger; in
  external enable, the tab says it is waiting for the trigger signals.
- **A refused setting says why,** in the detector's own words, instead of only
  "HTTP 400".

### Fixed

- **Files on a PILATUS4 are listed.** The tab said there were none.
- **The progress time no longer jumps** between decimals while a series runs.
- **"Locked while busy" is no longer cut off.**

---

Update if you use the beta Detector tab. Snap, Continuous and Open in ALBIS were
tried on a PILATUS4 and leave its settings as they were. The tab stays hidden
until you switch it on under Settings → Viewer.
