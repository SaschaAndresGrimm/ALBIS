A calmer beta Detector tab: leaner, nothing jumps while a series runs, and Stop works at every moment. Plus a fix for files opened with ALBIS while a live view runs.

### Changed

- **A leaner Detector tab that holds still.** Name, address, state and buttons
  take a few compact rows. The Acquisition header sums up the next series with
  its estimated size ("20 images · 20 s · ≈ 22 MB"), and the Data output header
  shows the free storage. Under the buttons, one line shows one thing at a
  time: what would make the series fail (in amber), its progress, the result,
  or a question such as "Acquire anyway?". Nothing above or below moves when it
  changes.
- **The buttons keep their shape.** Snap and Continuous sit beside Acquire as
  one block; while anything runs, Stop takes exactly their place and Acquire
  never changes size or position.
- **Live view on one line** with Snap's exposure and Continuous' rate.
  "Show images while acquiring" is now called **Live view**. Continuous runs for
  at most 10 hours, no longer a setting.
- **A tidier Acquisition section.** Each threshold's images switch sits left of
  its energy, every number field has the same width, and every **?** lines up
  in a column right of its field.

### Fixed

- **Stop during Snap or Continuous always stops them,** also while they are
  still setting up; what they changed is put back.
- **A stopped series says so:** "Series 108: stopped after 7.24 s", not the
  number of images it was set to.
- **Snap and Continuous no longer flash a warning** or make the buttons jump.
- **A file opened with ALBIS replaces a live source.** Double-clicking a file
  (or Open With) while the SIMPLON monitor was the data source kept the new
  window on the monitor; the file now becomes the source, as with File → Open.

---

Update if you use the beta Detector tab, or open files with ALBIS from your file
manager while watching a detector live. Snap's and Continuous' timings moved
from Advanced to the line under the buttons. The Detector tab stays hidden until
you switch it on under Settings → Viewer.
