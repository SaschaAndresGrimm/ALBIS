**Read Between the Pixels:** the numbers on the pixels now match the corrected image.

### Fixed

- **Pixel values follow flatfield correction and inpainting.** The values drawn
  on the pixels when zoomed in, and in PNG and GIF exports with pixel values,
  still showed the measured data while the image itself was corrected. They now
  show the values as drawn: flatfield-corrected, and filled where inpainting
  filled a pixel. Gaps, defective and saturated pixels are still marked as
  before.
- **The readout under the cursor shows both values** while flatfield correction
  or inpainting is on, for example "Value 0.437 (measured 1234)".
