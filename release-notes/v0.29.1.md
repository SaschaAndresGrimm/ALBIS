**Mind the Margin:** inpainting can now also fill the unreliable pixels around the flagged ones, and the frame number shows in full.

### Added

- **A margin around flagged pixels.** The pixels next to a gap or a defective
  pixel are often unreliable too. **Margin** (0–5 px) in the Inpainting
  section fills that many pixels around every flagged one as well, and the
  fill starts beyond them. It applies to the image, the ROI statistics and all
  exports; inpainted TIFF and CBF files say so in their header.

### Changed

- **A tidier Inpainting section.** Its options sit on one line, **Saturated
  pixels** and **Margin**, with the explanation behind a **(?)**.
- **Saturated pixels are filled by default.** Untick **Saturated pixels** to keep
  them as they are.

### Fixed

- **The frame number beside the slider shows in full.** Frame 2000 read as
  "200"; the box is now as wide as the series' largest frame number.

---

Update if you use inpainting, or work with series of more than 999 frames.
