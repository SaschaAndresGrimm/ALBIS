**True Colors:** every colour on the image now means what the colour bar says. Masked pixels get colours your colour map never uses, and a float image is drawn exactly as its histogram shows it.

### Security

- **certifi 2026.10.19.** The certificate bundle ALBIS uses for HTTPS trusted
  emSign root certificates whose intermediate keys were compromised
  ([GHSA-6q48-6c7w-9gw2](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-6q48-6c7w-9gw2)).
  They are removed.

### Fixed

- **A float image with a narrow contrast range is drawn as the colour bar
  shows it.** On a flatfield, a transmission map or a mean, the BG–FG range is
  often a fraction of a unit wide, and the image squeezed it onto part of the
  colour map. On Heat with FG at 0.73, a pixel of 1.0 was red on the image but
  white on the colour bar.
- **The colour bar fills the histogram's width on Retina and other
  high-resolution screens.** It used to show only its left half.

### Changed

- **Masked pixels get colours your colour map never uses.** Gaps, defective
  and saturated pixels were always black, dark blue and teal. On Viridis those
  sat on the scale itself. On Grey Scale, black gaps looked like the lowest
  values.
  - Now each keeps its usual colour where that stands out from the map, and is
    replaced where it doesn't. On Viridis, defective pixels are magenta and
    saturated ones red.
  - ALBULA HDR keeps its black gaps.
  - **View → Advanced view controls → Mask colours** sets any of them by hand.

### Added

- **A key for the mask colours** under the colour bar, showing the ones on
  screen.
- **The colour bar is a contrast control.** Drag it to move the BG–FG range,
  scroll on it to widen or narrow it, and double-click it for Auto Contrast.
