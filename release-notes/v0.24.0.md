Control a DECTRIS detector from ALBIS: the **beta Detector tab** sets up, starts and follows a series over SIMPLON, without any programming.

### Added

- **A Detector tab (beta), off until you switch it on.** Turn it on under
  Settings → Viewer → Beta: detector control, enter the detector's address and
  connect. It works with any DECTRIS detector that speaks SIMPLON 1.8.0 or later,
  and builds every field from what the detector itself reports: units, limits
  and choices are that detector's own.
- **One next step at a time.** The main button offers what makes sense now:
  Initialize after power-up, Acquire when ready, Stop while a series runs, with
  a progress bar, temperature, humidity and high voltage alongside.
- **Settings in the order you change them:** images per trigger, triggers and
  trigger mode; frame and count time; then energy and thresholds. A value
  outside the detector's range is refused before it is sent, and when the
  detector adjusts another setting to fit, the tab shows which.
- **A "?" beside each setting** explains it in plain words and names its SIMPLON
  key, the name to use when you later script the detector.
- **Watch the series as it is taken.** With **Show images while acquiring** (on
  by default), the viewer switches to the detector's live images as soon as the
  series is armed. It is a preview of the newest image; the complete data is in
  the files or the stream.
- **Files and data outputs.** Switch the file writer, stream and monitor on or
  off, see the name the next series will get and how much storage is left, and
  download the files from the detector, or open its data page.
- **Troubleshooting** in one place: re-initialize the detector, reset the
  stream, delete the files on the detector, each with a line on when to use it
  and a confirmation that says what it does. A hint appears by itself when high
  voltage is not ready, a command failed or storage runs low.
- **Works alongside a beamline control system.** Changes made by another
  program are flagged, and Abort always gets through.

### Changed

- **The electron-microscopy detectors ARINA, SINGLA, QUADRO and ELA** are listed
  as supported.
- **DECTRIS is named** on the splash screen ("developed at DECTRIS") and in
  Help → About, with the DECTRIS logo and the copyright line.

---

Update if you want to try ALBIS as a simple control panel for a DECTRIS detector:
the Detector tab is new and stays hidden until you switch it on under Settings →
Viewer. It is a beta, so feedback from your detector is welcome. Anyone who can
open ALBIS can use the tab while it is on, so leave it off on a shared instance
unless that is intended. Nothing changes for viewing data.
