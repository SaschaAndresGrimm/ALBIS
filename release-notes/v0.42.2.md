**Mostly Harmless**: the Guide's entire entry for Earth, and now what ALBIS's error messages aim for. 0.42.2 makes ALBIS's notifications calm, specific and out of the way: each says what went wrong and why, once, and none of them covers the status bar any more.

### Fixed

- **Notifications no longer cover the status bar.** They appear just above it,
  so the **SERVER** badge, **Versions** and **GitHub** stay visible, and they
  move up while the **Versions** popover is open. They are opaque, so the
  status bar no longer shows through a red error.
- **Each failure says why.** A second line gives the cause, such as
  "Frame 5: Cannot reach the server…". A failed frame or file load has a
  **Retry** button.
- **A failure is reported once.** It used to show in the notification and
  again in the status bar at the bottom left, where it stayed after the
  notification had gone. The status bar now says what ALBIS is doing and goes
  back to **Ready**. The same failure repeating, as during playback, stays one
  notification with a count (×12).
- **When the server stops answering, ALBIS says so.** Instead of a stream of
  "Failed to load frame" next to a faded **OFFLINE** badge, one notice reads
  "The ALBIS server is not responding. Retrying…", and ALBIS says when it is
  connected again.

---

Everyone can update; nothing about files, settings or the image changes. If
the server stops while you are working, look for the one notice above the
status bar rather than a pile of errors. Share and enjoy.
