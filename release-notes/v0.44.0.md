**One Ring to Rule Them All**: from Zelda's gift to the One Ring, as 0.44.0 is about rings. The Resolution Rings section can now draw the rings of a known material over the image, to check the geometry against a calibrant or to name a ring that should not be there. Lucas Wagner contributed again: Find bad pixels can now mask by value.

### Added

- **Reference rings for a calibrant, a metal or ice.** A new **Reference**
  menu under the ring list offers 22 materials: Si, LaB₆ and CeO₂, the
  small-angle calibrants silver behenate, tetradecanol and PBBA, Al, Au, Ni
  and Pt, oxides, minerals and hexagonal ice. Their rings are drawn solid and
  magenta, labelled with the material and hkl ("Al 111"), next to your own
  dashed ones. If a calibrant's measured rings sit on the drawn ones, the
  distance, energy and beam centre are right. The d-spacings come from
  pyFAI's calibrant library.
- **Find bad pixels can mask by value** (Pixel Mask, beta). The new rule
  **Pixels with value** takes every pixel that reads exactly a number, or
  that number or more, or less: every zero in a flat-field image, every pixel
  at the overflow value, or everything past a cutoff.

### Changed

- **Detector Geometry lines up with Data Source.** One row per value, the
  label on the left. A value missing from the metadata says so under its own
  field, and **Enter manually** switches the override on and puts you in the
  first missing field. **Reset to metadata** shows only while a manual value
  is in use.
- **Ice rings are a reference now.** The **Ice rings** button is gone;
  choose **H₂O (ice Ih)** at the top of the **Reference** menu. Ice no longer
  takes 10 of your 16 rings, and **Clear** sits next to **+ Add ring**.

### Fixed

- **The menu bar, toolbar and status bar stay opaque** while the mouse rests
  on the image. They faded far enough for a bright frame to colour them and
  for pixel values to show through the toolbar.
- **The pixel readout, rings, peaks, pixel values and ROI stay visible over
  the pixel mask's tint**, and **Loading frame…** is drawn above them too.
- **Edit on image takes a box over a whole EIGER2 16M frame.** An edit of
  more than 16.7 million pixels stopped with an error.

---

Everyone can update; files and settings are unchanged. If you used the
**Ice rings** button, the same rings are now under **Reference**; ice rings
you already added stay in your list until you clear them. Find bad pixels and
Edit on image are in Data → Pixel Mask, a beta switched on in Settings →
Tools.
