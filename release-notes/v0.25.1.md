A new face for ALBIS: a new start screen, a wordmark to match, and a fix for opening a series from the Detector tab.

### Changed

- **A new start screen.** ALBIS opens on a new picture with the DECTRIS
  wordmark, fading softly into the dark. The text below it is simpler: once
  ALBIS is ready, there is just the **Open file** button and the shortcut to
  press instead.
- **The ALBIS wordmark has its own typeface,** Michroma: wide and squared, in
  keeping with the DECTRIS wordmark. It is used on the start screen and in
  Help → About; the rest of the interface is unchanged.

### Fixed

- **Open in ALBIS switches to the file.** After watching a series live in the
  beta Detector tab, opening the recorded series now switches the data source
  to File, as File → Open does, instead of staying on the live view.

---

Update for the new look, or if you use Open in ALBIS in the beta Detector tab.
Nothing changes in how ALBIS opens or shows your data.
