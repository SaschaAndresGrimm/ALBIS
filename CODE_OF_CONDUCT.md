# Code of Conduct

ALBIS is a small project used by people who mostly meet each other through
beamtime, conferences and shared instruments. The point of this document is not
ceremony: it is so that someone who has a problem knows exactly who to tell and
what will happen next.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming bad faith.
- Focus on the work, not the person.
- Accept that people contribute at different levels of experience, in different
  languages, and around their actual jobs.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or sustained hostility.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or dismissing others' contributions in bad
  faith.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project occupies — issues, pull requests, commit
messages, code comments, discussions and the artefacts we publish — and to
conduct outside those spaces when it is directed at a participant *because* of
their involvement in ALBIS, or when it would make them unsafe here.

## Reporting

**Email the maintainer directly: <sascha.grimm@dectris.com>.** This address is
already public in [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff), so writing to it discloses
nothing about you that a reader could not already see.

Do **not** open a GitHub issue for a conduct report. Issues are public, and an
earlier version of this document asked people to report harassment where the
person they were reporting could read it. That was wrong.

A report is most useful with: what happened, where, roughly when, and whether
you want to be identified when it is raised with the other person. If you would
rather not write to the maintainer — because the report concerns him, or for any
other reason — raise it with someone you trust at your facility, or contact
GitHub Support, who can act on their own Terms of Service independently of this
project.

## What happens next

- You get an acknowledgement within **five working days**. If you do not, assume
  the mail went astray and send it again.
- The report is kept confidential. Details are shared with the person reported
  only to the extent needed to act on it, and only in a form you have agreed to.
- You are told the outcome.

Depending on severity, the response is one of:

1. **A private word** — an explanation of what was wrong and why, with no
   further action.
2. **A warning** — a request to stop, with the consequence of continuing made
   explicit.
3. **A temporary block** — from interaction in the project's spaces, for a
   stated period.
4. **A permanent ban** — from all project spaces, for sustained or severe
   behaviour.

Content that violates this code may be removed or edited at any point, with a
note that it was.

## A necessary limitation

ALBIS currently has one maintainer, so conduct reports are read and acted on by
one person, and there is no separate body to appeal to. That is a real limit
rather than an oversight, and it is written down here so nobody discovers it at
the worst moment. If the project gains maintainers, this section changes with
it. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
